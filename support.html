A few notes for the moments when a field note, recognition or park needs a little untangling.

✦

Recognition

The local model suggests animals from the supported launch catalog. Suggestions can be uncertain. Confirm the species manually before saving when needed.

▦

Collection

Your journal counts saved catches. The launch catalog includes 500 selected animal species; The recognition model supports part of this catalog; choose the species manually if it cannot identify your photo. Camera and photo-library catches both count toward your collection and rankings.

⌖

Locations

Location is optional per catch. Use Settings to hide saved coordinates from the journal and map views.

Community choices

Public profile sharing is opt-in. It can show your collector ID, display name, park and progress. Turn the toggle off in Community to remove the public profile and park snapshot tied to your iCloud account.

Private friend sharing uses Apple’s iCloud share sheet. A share can require an iCloud account and an available CloudKit container.

Still stuck?

Tell us what you expected, what you saw, and the iOS version. Please avoid sending private photos or location details.
