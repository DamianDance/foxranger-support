Privacy

Your field notes stay yours.

Your discoveries belong to you. Here is how FoxRanger handles your photos, saved progress and optional sharing.

Short version: photos and cutouts are processed and stored on the device. FoxRanger does not upload photo assets for its recognition flow.

What FoxRanger uses

  • Camera and photo library: only when you choose to capture or import an image. Recognition and sticker generation run locally using the bundled model.
  • Local files: catch photos, cutouts and the local game save are kept in the app’s local storage.
  • Optional location: you can choose to attach a location to a catch. You can also hide saved map coordinates in Settings. Location is not required for recognition.
  • Private iCloud: when iCloud is available and you choose cloud saving, CloudKit stores game and catch metadata in the app’s private container. This backup contains progress and catch metadata, without photo files, cutouts or catch coordinates. Apple operating-system backups may include app data according to your device settings.
  • Purchases: premium purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit. FoxRanger does not receive your payment details.

Optional community sharing

If you opt in, the app can publish a public profile containing your collector ID, display name, park name and park progress, plus leaderboard values. You can turn the profile private again in the app; that removes the public profile and park snapshot owned by your iCloud account.

Friend sharing uses private iCloud records and Apple’s native CloudKit share flow. It is separate from publishing a public profile.

Your choices and data

FoxRanger does not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. Local discoveries remain on your device until you remove them or delete the app. Private iCloud backups are separate from local storage. Contact us for help removing app-managed cloud data. Apple manages your iCloud identity and purchase records under its own privacy policy.

Questions or deletion requests

To ask about privacy or request help removing an app-managed public profile, email d.witko@icloud.com. Please do not send photos or sensitive information unless support asks for it.
